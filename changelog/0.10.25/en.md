## Login account preparation

Apps can prepare a person’s login account separately from invitation mail, preserving existing passwords and access. Adopt the matching SDK, Core and managed runtime before enabling consumers. See [backend contracts](https://developers.nexia.to/docs/app-sdk-sdk-contracts) and [identity contract source](https://github.com/nexia-cloud-os/app-sdk-laravel/tree/main/src/Identity).
