title: Company-scoped access termination
description: End company participation without deleting the person identity.
---

Apps can request company-scoped access termination through `PersonLegalEntityAccess.end`. Identity and other-company access remain intact; tenant-wide authority requires explicit review. Adopt Core 0.8.159 and Manager 0.1.0-alpha.83 before enabling callers. See the [contract and permissions guide](https://developers.nexia.to/docs/app-sdk-sdk-contracts#current-person-status-and-company-access-draft).
