title: Organization references in Composition queries

Composition providers can project Legal Entity and Operating Unit public IDs and labels with the Laravel SDK. Providers must continue to apply their own actor and organization authorization. See the [report query guide](https://developers.nexia.to/docs/app-sdk-governed-reports) and [SDK implementation](https://github.com/nexia-cloud-os/app-sdk-laravel/blob/v0.10.23/src/Laravel/ResourceComposition/OrganizationReferenceProjection.php).
