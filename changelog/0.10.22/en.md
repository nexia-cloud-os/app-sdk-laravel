Apps may declare editable default dashboards and sources that follow the viewer's Legal Entity selection. Personal sources can be independent of that selection. Declarations do not grant access. Adopt matching Core and App runtime support. See the [dashboard guide](https://developers.nexia.to/docs/platform-extensions-add-dashboard-widget) and [SDK source](https://github.com/nexia-cloud-os/app-sdk-laravel/tree/main/src/Dashboard).
