## App-owned profile tabs

Apps can declare complete profile screens with `ProfileTabDescriptor` without a Dashboard or slot widget declaration. Adopt the matching Core and App runtime before submitting the declaration. See the [profile tab guide](https://developers.nexia.to/docs/platform-extensions-add-profile-tab) and [SDK descriptor source](https://github.com/nexia-cloud-os/app-sdk-laravel/tree/main/src/AppDescriptors).
